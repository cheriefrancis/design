Origin

Inspired by a true story

This game is based on a real childhood memory — As a graduation tradition at my high school, students are each given a plant that represents how their future will grow. The idea is you nurture it, watch it grow alongside you.

// The inspiration

"A goat ate my friend's 'future'"

When my friend returned for sixth form, she told me a goat had walked into her yard and eaten her graduation plant. She was absolutely furious, but that image of a goat entering her yard and eating her future, never left me. This game is that moment, turned interactive.

Early concept — the yard and fence layout
Inspired by Pokémon and Stardew Valley's pixel warmth

Gameplay Story

The game follows a girl who just graduated high school, then immediately has to defend her future from a very hungry goat. The story unfolds in clear steps, each tied to a player interaction.

01

Receive the plant

Talk to an NPC who gifts you a plant, a symbol of your future growing alongside you. Press E to interact and read the dialogue.

Press E to interact
02

Plant it in your yard

Find the right spot and plant your graduation gift. It's yours to protect now.

Press E to plant
03

Collect wood and build a fence

Gather wood scattered around the yard to build a protective fence around your plant. Because you know something's coming.

Press E to collect
04

The goat arrives

A goat walks into the yard, knocks down the fence, and goes straight for the plant.

Cutscene
05

Chase the goat

Run the goat down across the yard. WASD to move. Press E to catch it when close enough.

WASD + Press E to catch
06

Get your revenge in the boxing ring

Originally a bug — the player could kick the goat and it would fly across the screen. Everyone loved it so much it became a minigame: you and the goat in a boxing ring to get revenge.

Bug → Feature ⭐

Game Design

Controls & interactions

Movement

  • WASD — move the character
  • Navigate between areas and chase the goat
  • Approach objects and NPCs to trigger interactions

Interactions

  • E — interact with NPCs, items, objects
  • E — catch the goat when close enough
  • Arrow / Next button — advance dialogue
  • E — plant the graduation plant
  • E — collect wood for fence building
