Step2Pet

A gamified step-tracking app that links your daily walking to a virtual pet, making fitness habitual and not pressured.

The problem

Exercise can feel like a chore

Young adults who work or study remotely struggle to stay physically active and without natural movement from commuting or campus life, daily step counts drop. Existing trackers feel clinical and guilt-driven, which kills long-term motivation rather than building it.

Research & insights

What we learned from users

Surveys, interviews, and diary studies with young adults revealed three consistent patterns that shaped the entire design direction.

Walking happens incidentally - Errands, commuting, social plans result in unintentional exercise

Motivation rises when walking feels rewarding, social, or visually engaging

Gamification and friendly competition increase engagement

These insights pointed toward a design focused on intrinsic motivation and habit-building rather than strict fitness goals.

Design decisions

Why things were built this way

Virtual pet as emotional hook

Pet care is unlocked only after step goals are met, directly linking physical activity to in-app progression. Creates a daily reason to return without guilt or pressure.

StepBucks reward system

In-app currency earned by exceeding goals and can be spent on pet customisation. Encourages incremental improvement rather than all-or-nothing thinking - missing a day doesn't feel like failure.

Friends-only leaderboard

Competition is limited to friends, not strangers. Adds light accountability while designed to feel fun, not judgmental.

Hand-crafted visual language

Pet and environment illustrations drawn in Procreate, while icons and accessories designed in Illustrator. Gives the app a warm, personal feel that is distinct from generic fitness apps.

Design system

Building the visual language

A full design system was developed after producing low-fidelity wireframes. The brand/design guidelines cover typography, colour, logo, UI components, and custom assets. This kept the app visually consistent across all states and flows.
